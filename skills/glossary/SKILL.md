# Booking terms, in plain English

**Booking reference** — the short code identifying your booking, usually six characters.
It is what to quote when asking about it. Airlines sometimes call it a PNR.

**Option** — seats held for you but not yet paid for. Held until a deadline, then
released. An option is not a confirmed trip. A booking made through the booking tool is an
option until its tickets are issued, and Getflights Pro shows how long it has left. A
flight Claude holds for you is the same kind of option.

**Ticketed** — tickets have been issued. This is the point at which travel is secure.

**Last ticketing date** — the date the airline needs tickets issued by. Miss it and the
seats can be released. The most important date on most bookings.

**E-ticket** — the electronic ticket for one traveller. Issued once the booking is
ticketed.

**Quote** — the offer made to you: flights, dates and price, before it was agreed.

**Sales order** — the agreed sale, once a quote is accepted. Invoices refer to it.

**Booking fee / transaction fee** — Getflights charges, separate from the airline's fare.

**Wallet** — money you paid in advance to Getflights, in one currency, that bookings are
paid from. Its balance is the *available amount*. Topping it up adds to it; a booking paid
from it takes money out.

**Deadline** — something you have to do on a booking by a given day: sign the contract,
pay the deposit or the rest, send the passenger names, or have the tickets issued.
*Urgent* means overdue or due within 5 days; *upcoming*, within 14.

**Cancellation moment** — the day from which cancelling a booking costs a given amount,
until the next moment takes over. A *partial* cancellation moment is about cancelling part
of the group.

**Deposit** — on an offer or a booking, the first payment agreed for a group, due before
the rest. Not the same thing as your wallet.

**Passport details required** — the airline needs passport information for every traveller
before departure. Worth dealing with early; it holds groups up.

**Infant / child / young adult** — airlines price these differently from adults, so a
booking counts them separately. The thresholds vary by airline.

## Terms you may hear from an agent

**GDS** — the airline reservation system a booking is held in.

**Coupon** — the part of a ticket covering one flight. A return trip has two.

**Validating carrier** — the airline that issued the ticket, which is not always the
airline flying every leg of the trip.
